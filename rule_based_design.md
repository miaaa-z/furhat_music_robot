# Part 2: Authoritative Article: Music and Human Behavioral Responses

### **A. Music and Emotional/Physical Responses**

#### 1. **"Music and emotion: Theory and research" (2001)**
- **Authors**: Juslin, P. N., & Sloboda, J. A.
- **Link**: https://academic.oup.com/book/53643
- **Relevant Content**:
  - Fast tempo → excitement, arousal → tendency for rapid movement
  - High intensity → increased arousal → large-scale movements
  - Minor mode → sadness → slow, closed body language

#### 2. **"Body expressions influence recognition of emotions in the face and voice." (2007)**
- **Authors**: Van den Stock, J., Righart, R., & de Gelder, B.
- **Link**: https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2007-11660-003
- **Key Points**:
  - Happy emotions → open posture, upward movements
  - Anger → expansive posture, rapid movements
  - Sadness → contracted posture, slow movements


### **B. Music Genre and Specific Behavior Mapping**

#### 3. **The song is you: Musical preferences predict personality (2016)**
- **Authors**: Greenberg, D. M., et al.
- **Link**:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/302595152_The_Song_Is_You_Preferences_for_Musical_Attribute_Dimensions_Reflect_Personality
- **Genre Characteristics**:

| Genre | Personality Traits | Typical Behavioral Response |
|-------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **Jazz** | Openness, intellectual | Contemplative, head nodding, eyes closed appreciation |
| **Rock** | Rebellious, energetic | Head shaking, fist pumping, jumping |
| **Classical** | Introverted, calm | Elegant swaying, eyes closed immersion |
| **Pop** | Extroverted, upbeat | Clapping, bouncing, smiling |
| **Blues** | Melancholic, soulful | Slow swaying, head down |


### **C. Genre-Specific Research**
JAZZ
#### 4. : "You Can't Dance to It": Jazz Music and Its Choreographies of Listening (2021)
- **Link**: https://www.amacad.org/publication/you-cant-dance-jazz-music-choreographies-listening
- **Key Findings**:
  - Jazz transition from dance music → art music
  - Modern jazz audience behavior:
    - **Seated, attentive listening**
    - **Modest head nodding or foot tapping** to the beat
    - Applause after solos
    - Less physical movement compared to rock/pop

ROCK
#### 5. : "Elements of musical and dance sophistication predict musical groove perception" (2022)
- **Link**: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.998321/full
- **Key Findings** (citing Luck et al., 2010):
  - **Extraverted listeners** to high-groove genres (rock, jazz, Latin, techno, funk, pop):
    - Greater **local and global body movements**
    - Faster **head speeds**
    - Greater **hand flux and hand distance**
    
#### 6. "The Social Psychology of Heavy Metal & Rock Music"
- **Link**: https://cognitiontoday.com/the-social-psychology-of-heavy-metal-rock-music-research-on-metalheads/
- **Listener Behaviors**:
  - **Headbanging**, entering mosh pits
  - **Raising fists in the air**
  - **Devil's horns hand gesture** \m/
  - State of **flow** during performances

POP
#### 7. : "Keeping the Beat: A Large Sample Study of Bouncing and Clapping to Music" (2016)
- **Authors**: Multiple researchers
- **Link**: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0160178
- **Study Sample**: 100 university students
- **Key Findings**:
  - **Clapping** better synchronized with beat than bouncing
  - Performance improved with **beat saliency**
  - Tested with: Pop Dance, Pop Rock, Soul stimuli
  - Pop music facilitates **synchronized clapping and bouncing**
  - Natural human reactions: **bouncing, clapping to beat**


### **D. Cross-Modal Mapping Between Color and Music**

#### 8.Music–color associations are mediated by emotion" (2013)**
- **Authors**: Palmer, S. E., Schloss, K. B., Xu, Z., & Prado-León, L. R.
- **Link**: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1212562110
- **Study**: 18 classical orchestral music pieces (Bach, Mozart, Brahms)
- **Key Findings**:
  - **Fast + Major mode music** → Saturated, Lighter, Yellower colors
  - **Slow + Minor mode music** → Desaturated, Darker, Bluer colors
  - **Emotional mediation**: Music-to-color associations mediated by emotion (r = 0.89-0.99)
  - US and Mexican participants showed similar patterns (cross-cultural validation)
